Research & Professional Involvement

ADMI Symposium 2026 - Research & Professional Involvement

Participated in the ADMI 2026 Symposium, a conference focused on advancing diversity in computing and supporting academic and professional development for minority students in STEM.

During the symposium, I co-presented research titled "Artificial Intelligence and the Expanding Digital Divide" alongside a fellow Winston-Salem State University student. Our research explored how the rapid growth of artificial intelligence can both create new opportunities and widen gaps in access to technology, education, and digital resources.

In addition to presenting, I participated in a Capture the Flag (CTF) cybersecurity challenge, collaborated with peers on technical problem-solving activities, and attended AI-focused workshops and professional development sessions.

This experience strengthened my skills in research, technical communication, teamwork, and problem-solving, while also expanding my knowledge of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity. It also provided valuable opportunities to connect with students, faculty, and industry professionals.
